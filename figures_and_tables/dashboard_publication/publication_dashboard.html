Reading this honestly

  • The two power strategies are not two estimates of one thing. n=30000 beats n=200000 because a smaller sampling GWAS concentrates the draw on the strongest loci — the ones also detectable in the simulated 8,000-person study. Each answers “which loci would a study of size X report?”, and the answer moves with how selective X is.
  • Paired vs unpaired is confounded. Those two arms share only about half their causal loci (14–16% for the human categories), because the paired arm draws from the GWAS∩eQTL pool and the unpaired one from the full pool. The difference between them is mostly which loci were drawn, not the pairing rule. Unpartnered loci do sit in the denominator and can never contribute a hit, but that drag measures only about 0.45 points.
  • Cattle replicates are nested. Each block of five shares one ancestry, so cattle has far fewer independent populations than its replicate count suggests. Estimate cattle between-population variance at the block level, not the replicate level.
  • Arms are unbalanced right now. causal_power_n30000 pools 10 replicates where the others pool 15; those cells are marked. Cross-strategy differences carry that difference in precision.
