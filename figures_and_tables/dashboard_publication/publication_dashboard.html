Allele frequency by selection strength

Minor allele frequency of every variant in the GWAS panel, split by its selection coefficient. Box is the interquartile range with the median; whiskers run to the extremes. One panel per category × sampling strategy, each on its own y-axis. Non-neutral means selco ≠ 0, the pool causal loci are drawn from; causative means the variant was actually assigned a trait effect. Under the synthetic DFE (the background-selection categories) causative variants are drawn from the neutral pool, so their boxes sit in the 0 bin.
